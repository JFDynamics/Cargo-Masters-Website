Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how C.M.M Cargo Masters Marine Services Ltd ("Cargo Masters", "we", "us") handles personal data you give us through this website, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the applicable law of the Republic of Cyprus.

Who we are

C.M.M Cargo Masters Marine Services Ltd, 9 Karaiskakis Street, Agia Triada, 3032 Limassol, Cyprus. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. You can contact us about privacy at info@cargomastersms.com or on +357 25 585017.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the contact form, we receive the details you enter:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • the service you are interested in
  • your message

If you email or phone us directly, we receive the details you choose to share in that message or call. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you decide to work with us, to prepare and deliver our services. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and, where relevant, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract with you. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing without your consent.

Who processes it for us

This website and its contact form are hosted by Netlify, Inc., which stores form submissions on our behalf. Netlify may process data outside the European Economic Area; where it does, the transfer is covered by safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We share your data with no one else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any business relationship that follows, and for any period required by law. After that, we delete them.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact us at info@cargomastersms.com.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus (www.dataprotection.gov.cy).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
